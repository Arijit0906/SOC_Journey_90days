# Threat Intelligence Source

A Threat Intelligence Source is any platform, report, feed, or dataset that provides information about cyber threats, attacker activity, IOCs, vulnerabilities, or TTPs.

## 1. OSIENT

OSINT (Open Source Intelligence) is intelligence collected from publicly available sources.

### Examples
- Security blogs
- Research articles
- Public malware reports
- GitHub repositories
- News articles
- Social media

### Purpose
- Detect threats faster
- Enrich alerts
- Understand attacker behavior
- Improve threat hunting

### Limitations
✘ Quality varies

✘ Requires verification

---

### Examples include:

#### CERT-In (Indian Computer Emergency Response Team)

# What is a TIP?

A Threat Intelligence Platform (TIP) is a centralized system used to collect, store, analyze, correlate, and share threat intelligence.

## Simple Definition

A TIP helps security teams manage threat intelligence efficiently in one place.

Think of it as a SIEM for Threat Intelligence.

## Benefits of TIPs

### Centralized Intelligence
Everything in one location.

### Faster Investigations
Analysts get context quickly.

### Better Threat Hunting
Correlated indicators reveal patterns.

### Improved Detection
Intelligence can be pushed into security tools.

### Collaboration
Teams can share intelligence easily.

MISP (Malware Information Sharing Platform) is one of the most popular open-source Threat Intelligence Platforms.

### Primary Purpose

- Intelligence collaboration
