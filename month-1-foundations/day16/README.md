Today I went deeper into the **MITRE ATT&CK Framework** and explored its full scope.

---

## 📝 What I Learned
- Covered all **13 tactics** in the ATT&CK Enterprise Matrix.
- Explored **ATT&CK Navigator** for mapping detections.
- Learned about **ATT&CK Groups** and how adversaries are tracked.
- Studied **ATT&CK Software** entries (malware, tools).
- Compared **MITRE ATT&CK vs Kill Chain** models.

---

## 💡 Key Takeaways
- MITRE ATT&CK provides a **detailed, real-world mapping** of attacker behavior.
- Tactics explain attacker objectives, while techniques show how they achieve them.
- Navigator helps SOC teams visualize coverage and gaps.
- Groups and software entries connect techniques to actual adversaries.
- Kill Chain is linear, while ATT&CK is **matrix-based and more practical** for detection.
